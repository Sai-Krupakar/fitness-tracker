# Real-Life Level-Up Fitness Content Roadmap

## 1. Project Concept

Create short-form fitness videos inspired by the progression feeling of *Solo Leveling*, while keeping the workouts original, realistic, and safe.

The channel follows one real person starting at Level 1 and gradually improving through outdoor bodyweight workouts. Viewers participate by suggesting exercises, voting on missions, and sharing their own progress.

**Core promise:**

> Realistic outdoor fitness for ordinary people starting from zero.

The anime is inspiration only. The channel must not present itself as official *Solo Leveling* content.

## 2. Main Content Series

### Main series: Real-Life Training Arc

Each episode documents the current level, workout, progress, and next target.

### Community series: A-Z Weekly Missions

Every week is assigned one alphabet letter. Viewers comment safe exercises beginning with that letter. The most-liked suitable suggestion becomes the community mission.

### Engagement series: Top Comment Controls My Workout

Viewers choose one part of a future workout, such as the exercise, location, duration, or difficulty. The creator selects the most popular safe suggestion.

### Progress series: Beat My Score

The creator records a repeatable result, such as push-ups, plank time, or a 1 km run/walk. Viewers can attempt the same test and comment their result.

## 3. Level System

Train whenever your schedule allows. A level is earned through accumulated training days and good form, not by rushing to a larger number. There is no requirement to train every day or every week.

### Configurable plan duration

At setup, choose how long the complete plan should last. The app divides the selected duration across the eight ranks and rounds up to whole training weeks per rank:

| Plan duration | Approximate training days required at each rank | Approximate total |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 month | 3 days per rank | 22 training days |
| 3 months | 9 days per rank | 65 training days |
| 6 months | 17 days per rank | 130 training days |
| 9 months | 25 days per rank | 196 training days |
| 12 months | 33 days per rank | 261 training days |

The selected duration sets a target number of flexible training days. The user can train on any dates and does not lose points for taking breaks. A training day is counted once when at least one exercise quantity is logged on that date.

| Level | Push-ups | Squats | Core exercise | Run/walk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1: Awakening | 20 x 3 sets | 20 x 3 sets | 20 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 2 km |
| 2: Recruit | 22 x 3 sets | 22 x 3 sets | 22 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 2.5 km |
| 3: Hunter | 25 x 3 sets | 25 x 3 sets | 25 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 3 km |
| 4: Elite | 28 x 3 sets | 28 x 3 sets | 28 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 4 km |
| 5: Commander | 30 x 3 sets | 30 x 3 sets | 30 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 5 km |
| 6: Monarch | 25 x 4 sets | 35 x 3 sets | 35 controlled repetitions x 3 sets | 6 km |
| 7: Shadow Monarch | 25 x 4 sets | 25 x 4 sets | 25 controlled repetitions x 4 sets | 8 km |
| 8: National Level | 100 total | 100 total | 100 total core repetitions | 10 km |

### Level-up rule

Move up only after completing the duration-based number of training days at the current level, with controlled form, adequate recovery, and no pain. The selected plan duration determines the total training-day target.

If a level is too difficult, repeat it, reduce the repetitions, or use an easier variation. A missed target becomes a story point, not a failure to hide:

> Training mission incomplete. Retrying Level 2 next week.

### Exercise variations

- Push-ups: regular, incline, knee, or negative push-ups
- Squats: bodyweight squats, box squats, or supported squats
- Core: plank, dead bug, curl-up, or controlled leg raise
- Running: brisk walking, run/walk intervals, or easy continuous running

Level 1 is your current starting point, based on the capacity you reported. The 10 km and 100-repetition targets remain milestones rather than daily requirements. From Level 6 onward, progress can come from better form, harder variations, shorter rest, or improved running pace instead of endlessly adding repetitions.

## 4. Weekly Schedule

| Day | Content and activity |
|---|---|
| Monday | Main workout: current level |
| Tuesday | Main workout: current level |
| Wednesday | Main workout: current level; use a lighter variation if needed |
| Thursday | Main workout: current level |
| Friday | Main workout: current level and weekly progress check |
| Saturday | A-Z mission: complete the winning viewer suggestion and post the result |
| Sunday | Full rest day; review results and prepare the next week's mission |

The community mission should be short and should add variety, not turn into punishment. Keep it to one exercise or a brief technique challenge. Use Sunday for recovery rather than an additional workout.

## 5. A-Z Mission Framework

Use one letter per week. Ask viewers to comment a safe movement beginning with that letter. The creator chooses the winning suggestion based on popularity, suitability, and safety.

| Letter | Example mission |

| B | Beginner burpees |
| C | Calf raises |
| D | Diamond push-up progression |
| E | Explosive step-ups |
| F | Frog jumps, if suitable for the participant |
| G | Glute bridges |

| L | Lunges |
| M | Mountain climbers |

| O | Outdoor walking challenge |
| P | Push-ups |

## 6. Standard Video Structure

Keep each short between approximately 20 and 45 seconds when the story can be told clearly.

1. **Hook, 0-2 seconds:** State the challenge immediately.
2. **Level card, 2-5 seconds:** Show the level and today's target.
3. **Workout clips, 5-25 seconds:** Show clear form and brief exercise labels.
4. **Progress result, 25-35 seconds:** Show completed repetitions, distance, or time.
5. **Audience prompt, final seconds:** Ask one specific question.

### Example script

> Day 1, Level 1. I am starting with 20 push-ups, 20 squats, 20 core repetitions, and a 2 km run/walk, all for three sets where applicable. Comment an exercise beginning with A. The safest popular suggestion becomes this week's bonus mission. Follow the journey and try your own Level 1.

### Weekly recap script

> Level 1 week complete. I finished four of five planned workouts, so I am repeating this level instead of rushing ahead. The winning A mission was agility drills. Comment B exercises for next week, and share your result if you trained with me.

## 7. Comment and Community Rules

Ask one clear question per video:

- What A exercise should I try?
- Which version should I do: 10, 20, or 30 repetitions?
- Did you complete your Level 1 today?
- What should I train at the park next week?

Use comments to create follow-up videos. Pin the winning suggestion, reply to useful questions, and feature viewer progress only with permission.

Do not repeatedly tag unwilling people. The content should continue even if a tagged person does not reply. Never shame someone for not participating, and do not use fake comments, bought likes, or fake giveaways.

## 8. Brand and Platform Plan

### Suggested identity

Use a memorable name such as `comment_guy` only if the channel's main promise is clear. A possible display name is **Comment Guy: Level-Up Fitness**.

### Bio example

> Starting fitness from Level 1. Outdoor workouts, viewer missions, and honest progress. Train with me.

### Initial platforms

Post original vertical versions to:

- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- Facebook Reels
- Moj, Josh, or ShareChat for regional-language reach

TikTok is not officially available in India, so do not bypass restrictions or use unofficial versions.

Use 9:16 vertical video, readable captions, clear audio, and platform-approved music. Keep the original video file so every platform can receive a clean export.

## 9. Monetization Roadmap

Do not build the first months around income. Build trust and repeat viewers first.

Possible income sources include:

1. YouTube ads and eligible creator features
2. Instagram and Facebook brand collaborations
3. Fitness equipment, clothing, or shoe affiliate links
4. Paid coaching, only with suitable knowledge and qualifications
5. Beginner workout PDFs or structured programs
6. Live-stream memberships, gifts, Stars, or similar features
7. Sponsored challenges

Disclose paid promotions clearly using labels such as `#Ad` or `#Sponsored`. Keep records of income and check Indian income-tax and GST obligations with a qualified professional as earnings grow.

## 10. Safety and Copyright Checklist

Before posting each video:

- Warm up and use controlled form.
- Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual breathlessness.
- Increase distance and repetitions gradually.
- Take rest days and sleep adequately.
- Do not promise guaranteed weight loss, muscle gain, or medical results.
- Encourage viewers with injuries or health conditions to seek professional advice.
- Use footage, music, logos, and artwork that you created or have permission to use.
- Do not use anime clips, official logos, or copyrighted music as channel identity without permission.
- Describe the content as **Solo Leveling-inspired**, not official.

## 11. First 30 Days

### Week 1: Awakening

- Introduce the concept and Level 1.
- Complete five Level 1 workouts.
- Start the A mission: ask viewers for safe exercise suggestions.
- Post one progress update and one beginner-form tip.

### Week 2: Recruit

- Repeat or advance based on the level-up rule.
- Run the B mission selected from comments.
- Post a Beat My Score test.
- Reply to one viewer comment with a video.

### Week 3: Community training

- Let viewers choose the location or workout order.
- Run the C mission.
- Feature viewer results with permission.
- Show an honest setback or adjustment if one occurs.

### Week 4: First evaluation

- Run the D mission.
- Compare Day 1 with Day 28.
- Share sessions completed, total distance, and personal improvements.
- Ask viewers whether the next month should focus on strength, running, or mobility.

## 12. Tracking Sheet

Record these after every post and workout:

- Date and platform
- Level and mission letter
- Exercise and target
- Workout completed: yes or no
- Repetitions, time, and distance
- Views
- Average watch time or retention
- Comments, saves, shares, and new followers
- Top viewer suggestion
- What to improve next time

Judge the channel by returning viewers, comments, saves, shares, and completed community workouts, not views alone.

## 13. The Long-Term Story

The channel's story is:

> Start from Level 1, train consistently, let the community choose weekly missions, document setbacks honestly, and gradually work toward the long-term challenge.

The audience should feel that they are not merely watching a workout. They are helping choose the next mission and progressing alongside the creator.
